About

Quiet infrastructure, done carefully.

I'm an early-career data engineer in Tampere, Finland, finishing an MSc in Computer Science and Electrical Engineering at Tampere University. My day-to-day is building ETL/ELT workflows, benchmarking storage formats, and exploring batch and streaming systems on Databricks.

What pulled me into this field is the link between data quality and everything downstream — models, dashboards, decisions. Good data work is invisible when it's right and expensive when it's wrong, and I like being responsible for that difference.

Right now I'm deepening my Azure and Delta Lake practice and looking for a team in Finland where I can own pipelines end to end.
