I am building this portfolio to document my growth as an early-career data engineer.

My focus is on practical projects that show how raw data becomes useful data: extraction, transformation, storage, validation, and documentation. I am especially interested in Python, SQL, PySpark, Databricks, Delta Lake, and cloud data platforms.

A good portfolio is not only a list of tools. It should show decisions, trade-offs, and lessons learned from building data workflows.
